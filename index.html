Three real patterns, anonymized. Each shows the original bullet, the JD-mirrored rewrite, and what changed structurally.

DevOps Engineer → Platform Engineer (title + scope match)

title mirroring
− Managed Kubernetes clusters and CI/CD pipelines for the team.
+ Owned platform reliability for 12 microservices on EKS; rebuilt CI/CD to cut deployment failures 40%.
Result: the JD used "Platform Engineer" and "reliability" repeatedly; the candidate's real scope already matched — the rewrite made that overlap explicit instead of leaving it implied.

NIST 800‑53 background → FedRAMP‑adjacent role

transferable skill
− Familiar with security compliance frameworks.
+ Implemented NIST 800‑53 controls across cloud infrastructure; directly transferable to FedRAMP control families.
Result: genuine transferable experience, stated with enough precision that a compliance-focused screener can map it themselves — no claim of FedRAMP experience the candidate didn't have.

TPM resume, re-ordered for a fintech JD

JD re-ordering
− Responsible for cross-functional coordination across engineering teams.
+ Ran the release process across 4 engineering teams for a payments platform, cutting release cycle time from 3 weeks to 9 days.
Result: same underlying work, but quantified and re-ordered to the top third of the resume, matching the fintech JD's language on release velocity and payments-domain context.
