Gap check tool

Compare a job posting with your resume or experience notes. This free tool scans for shared terms and phrases, then shows likely matches and gaps. It's a simple rule-based scan, not a human review or a hiring prediction. Your text stays in your browser and isn't sent to us.

Step 1 of 2

Used only to check whether similar title language appears in your text.

Paste at least a few sentences — a single line usually isn't enough to extract anything useful.

Paste at least a few sentences of real experience to compare against.

What this tool can't tell you

A phrase match can't tell whether two experiences are truly equivalent, and the scan may miss terms it doesn't recognize. It also can't weigh context the way a person can; that's part of the full service. See how it works or compare packages.
