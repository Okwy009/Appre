Referral program

If you refer someone

Once the person you referred completes a Core or Complete engagement, you get a $50 credit toward a future package or a friend's engagement, transferable to anyone you choose. Credits don't expire and there's no cap on how many people you can refer.

If you were referred

Mention your referrer's name or email when you reach out. You get 15% off your first package, applied before payment — it stacks with nothing else, but it's automatic once we confirm the referral.

These are the actual, current terms of the program, not a promotional estimate — if they change, this page changes with them.
